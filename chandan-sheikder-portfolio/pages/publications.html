Nature Portfolio Published · 2025

Bio-inspired navigation systems for robots

C. Sheikder, et al. · Nature Reviews Bioengineering (2025). DOI: 10.1038/s44222-025-00367-6

Comprehensive review synthesizing biological navigation strategies observed in insects, terrestrial mammals, and marine organisms with computational robotic implementations. Evaluates multi-scale physiological models, neural path integration circuits, and event-based sensory fusion for GPS-denied autonomous operation.

Key Editorial Insights
  • Formulates closed-loop cross-domain bio-inspired mapping between biological neural substrates and robotic SLAM stacks.
  • Identifies low-power neuromorphic architectures achieving up to 80% computational energy reduction during agile locomotion.

Cell Press Published · 2025

Neuromorphic navigation for autonomous robots

C. Sheikder, et al. · Device (Cell Press, 2025). DOI: 10.1016/j.device.2025.100998

Explores silicon retina event cameras and neuromorphic spiking neural networks (SNNs) for instantaneous landmark extraction and trajectory generation. Demonstrates substantial latency and power advantages over traditional frame-based vision SLAM on edge robotics compute.

Device Level Innovations
  • Hardware-software co-design executing low-latency spike-driven obstacle avoidance under ≤1.5W budget.
  • Sub-millisecond latency tracking during dynamic visual degradation and rapid camera rotations.
