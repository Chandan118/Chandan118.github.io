1. Neuromorphic Navigation & Insect-Inspired Sensory Processing

Traditional robotic vision pipelines rely on power-hungry, fixed-frame-rate cameras that induce motion blur and high latency. Our research translates the biological neural architecture of insect central complex (CX) circuits—specifically ring-attractor heading compasses and optic-flow path integration—into neuromorphic hardware using event cameras (DVS) and spiking neural networks (SNNs).

Scientific Contributions
  • Formulated asynchronous event-driven SLAM algorithms reducing processing latency to sub-millisecond scales.
  • Achieved over 80% reduction in computational power compared to conventional visual-inertial odometry.
  • Validated on ultra-low-power micro-robot platforms navigating cluttered obstacle fields.

2. Marine-Inspired Multimodal Sensor Fusion & Amphibious Autonomy

Aquatic and turbid subterranean environments completely incapacitate classical optical SLAM due to light scattering, suspended matter, and lack of distinct visual features. Drawing inspiration from the lateral-line organ of blind cavefish and seal vibrissae, we design multimodal sensor arrays integrating differential pressure/flow sensors, inertial measurement units (IMUs), and low-light event vision.

Key Results & Architecture
  • Developed Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) and neural residual estimators reconciling asynchronous hydrodynamic and visual telemetry.
  • Continuous metric state estimation maintained even when visual feedback drops below 5% fidelity.

3. Soft Computing & Tethered Robotic Exploration in Extreme Environments

Tethered exploration robots enable continuous multi-kilowatt power and high-bandwidth telemetry into extreme environments (e.g. radioactive facilities, vertical disaster shafts, deep pipelines), but face high risks of cable entanglement and snagging. We introduced a soft computing framework combining neuro-fuzzy inference systems with catenary shape prediction to guarantee clearance envelopes.

Field Robotics Impact
  • Closed-loop tension control and dynamic anchor-point mapping prevents cable entrapment around complex geometric obstacles.
  • Empirically validated in industrial pipeline inspection and dynamic confined arena setups.

4. Interplanetary Communication Latency & Radiation-Resilient Cognitive AI

Deep space exploration rovers operate under one-way communication latency spanning 3 to 22 minutes, precluding real-time Earth teleoperation. Concurrently, cosmic galactic radiation causes single-event upsets (SEUs) in deep learning neural weights. We developed fault-tolerant distributed decision trees and self-healing weight checkpoints that maintain safe rover traverse autonomously.

5. Bio-Inspired & Soft Robotics for Autonomous Wind Energy Operations

Offshore and onshore wind turbine blade inspection and maintenance traditionally rely on hazardous human rope access under adverse aerodynamic conditions. We formulate bio-inspired and soft robotic adhesion, locomotion, and non-destructive evaluation (NDE) frameworks, integrating robotic maintenance co-design directly into turbine architecture to minimize Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) and operational risk.

Key Results & Architecture
  • Formulated biomimetic adhesion and climbing locomotion mechanisms capable of adhering to curved, oscillating turbine surfaces.
  • Developed automated non-destructive evaluation (NDE) and ultrasonic inspection architectures for blade integrity verification.
  • Established a turbine-robot co-design paradigm for autonomous in situ maintenance in extreme marine environments.

EXPERIMENTAL PROTOCOLS & RIGOR

Nature Methods: Experimental Standards & Testbeds

Reproducible simulation-to-reality pipelines and empirical benchmark protocols adopted across our investigations.

Simulation Fidelity

  • Physics Engines: ROS 2 Humble/Iron with Gazebo Fortress and Webots co-simulation for multi-agent contact mechanics.
  • Synthetic Pheromone Fields: Continuous 2D/3D reaction-diffusion PDE solvers integrated as ROS 2 costmap plugins.
  • Domain Randomization: Varied terrain friction (μ = 0.2 to 0.9), dynamic optical illumination shifts, and simulated sensor noise.

Hardware Benchmarks

  • Edge Compute: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, Raspberry Pi 5, and custom STM32 motor co-processors.
  • Event Sensors: Prophesee Gen4.1 HD event-based vision sensors coupled with 9-axis micro-IMUs.
  • Power Profiling: Real-time INA219 current shunt monitors measuring sub-milliwatt idle and locomotion power profiles.

Validation Envelopes

  • Absolute Trajectory Error (ATE): Monitored via OptiTrack 12-camera motion capture ground truth (sub-millimeter precision).
  • Swarm Scalability: Validated across cohorts ranging from 1 to 50 agent nodes in dynamic maze arenas.
  • Fault Injection: Benchmarked under 20% to 50% agent communication loss and sensor failure rates.
