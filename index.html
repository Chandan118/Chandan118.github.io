Chandan Sheikder

Graduate Research Assistant at the Beijing Institute of Technology
I research bio-robotics, autonomous navigation, sensor fusion, and neuromorphic methods. My work focuses on developing robust navigation frameworks for GPS-denied environments through sensor fusion, neuromorphic approaches, and advanced simulation. To learn more about my projects please visit my personal website.
