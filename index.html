Chandan Sheikder

Graduate Research Assistant at the Beijing Institute of Technology.
I research bio-robotics, autonomous navigation, sensor fusion, and neuromorphic methods. My work focuses on developing robust navigation frameworks for GPS-denied environments through sensor fusion, neuromorphic approaches, and advanced multi-agent simulations.

To explore our thematic research pillars and open-source testbeds, please visit our Research Atlas and Flagship Projects.
